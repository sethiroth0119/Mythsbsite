Mythic Spellbook · New America

What is this?

This is an interactive map that gives players and fans the lore of the Mythic Spellbook universe. Explore the world, hover a site to read its story brief, and open it to read the comic issue behind it.

New America is a way for players to earn rewards they can redeem in the game — just for keeping up with the lore.

Read the story, claim the reward. Finish an issue and you unlock a redemption code. You might find loot and cards you will never find in the game anywhere else.

Drag to pan · scroll to zoom · click a glowing seal to begin.
